**Designing heuristic for firebreak selection**

I’m building a plan to select forest cells as firebreaks near towns to block fire paths from ignitions within a cut budget. I’ll use multi-source BFS from ignitions to compute fire arrival times, then trace shortest paths from towns back to ignitions to identify candidate cells weighted by town value and proximity, including neighbors for continuous breaks. After aggregating weights, I’ll pick top cells up to the budget, ensuring they’re burnable forest. This heuristic balances urgency and coverage without heavy combinatorial simulation.
