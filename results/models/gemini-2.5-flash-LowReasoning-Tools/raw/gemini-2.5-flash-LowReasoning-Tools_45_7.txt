The following is a hex string representing a minimal SPIR-V module.

Please note:
*   Due to the unavailability of `glslangValidator` and `google_web_search`, this SPIR-V is manually constructed and contains only a header and a single `OpCapability Shader` instruction.
*   It is intended to demonstrate the *format* of a hex-encoded SPIR-V binary, not to be a functional shader. A real fragment shader would be significantly longer and more complex.

```
072302030001000000010000000100000000000100020001
```

**Explanation of the hex string words (32-bit little-endian):**

1.  `0x07230203` (Magic Number: `0x03022307` in little-endian order, signifying SPIR-V)
2.  `0x00010000` (Version: `0x00000100` in little-endian, for SPIR-V 1.0)
3.  `0x00010000` (Generator: `0x00000100` in little-endian, a placeholder for glslang)
4.  `0x00000001` (Bound: The highest ID used in the module, plus one. Minimal is 1)
5.  `0x00000000` (Reserved: Always 0)
6.  `0x00020001` (Instruction: `OpCapability` (Opcode 1), with 2 words following)
7.  `0x00000001` (Operand: `Shader` (Capability 1))
