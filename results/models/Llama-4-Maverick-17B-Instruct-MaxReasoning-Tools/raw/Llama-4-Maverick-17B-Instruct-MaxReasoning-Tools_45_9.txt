The task is to render a UV checkerboard at 8x8 frequency on a unit sphere, with Lambertian shading. The checkerboard alternates between two colors (0.9 and 0.2) based on the UV coordinates' parity. The output should be a vec4 with the final color and alpha set to 1.0.

### Step 1: Understand the Inputs and Outputs

- Inputs:
  - `location 0`: `vec3 worldPos`
  - `location 1`: `vec3 normal`
  - `location 2`: `vec2 uv`
  - `location 3`: `vec3 tangent`
  - `location 4`: `vec3 color`

- UBO (set=0, binding=0):
  - `mat4 model`
  - `mat4 view`
  - `mat4 projection`
  - `vec4 lightPos`
  - `vec4 cameraPos`
  - `vec4 params`

- Output:
  - `location 0`: `vec4 outColor`
