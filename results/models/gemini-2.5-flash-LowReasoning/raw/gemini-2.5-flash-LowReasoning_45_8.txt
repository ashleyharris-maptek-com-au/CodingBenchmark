```glsl
#version 450
layout(location = 0) out vec4 outColor;

    outColor = vec4(1.0, 0.0, 0.0, 1.0); // Red color

```

    ```bash
    glslangValidator -V shader.frag -o shader.spv
    ```

    *   **Using `xxd` (Linux/macOS):**
        ```bash
        xxd -p -c 256 shader.spv
        ```
    *   **Using Python:**
        ```python
        with open("shader.spv", "rb") as f:
            hex_data = f.read().hex()
        ```
